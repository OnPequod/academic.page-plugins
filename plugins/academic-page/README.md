# Academic.page

Connects your agent to Academic.page, at https://academic.page/mcp. With this
plugin, Claude, ChatGPT, or Codex can read your site, including drafts. Give
it your CV, and it can add the publications, talks, positions, and degrees on
it to your site. Give it a syllabus, and it can set up a course. The first
time it connects, sign in to Academic.page in your browser, choose a site, and
choose the agent's access. See https://academic.page/manual/ai-apps for
details.
